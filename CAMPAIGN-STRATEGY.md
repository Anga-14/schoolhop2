# SchoolHop Digital Campaign Strategy

## Campaign purpose

SchoolHop Foundation helps rural learners in South Africa's Eastern Cape travel to school. The campaign's intended action is to grow qualified enquiries and completed sponsorship applications, while communicating transparently and safeguarding learner dignity.

## Objective

Increase completed sponsorship applications by 30% over six months compared with the preceding six-month baseline. Confirm baseline counts, attribution rules, and qualified-application criteria before launch.

## Audience and persona

**Primary:** South African corporate CSR/ESG decision-makers seeking accountable education/community partnerships. They need clear route costs, credible evidence, a point of contact, and outcome reporting.

**Secondary:** individual donors and community supporters; educators and local organisations who can refer communities and coordinate routes.

**Persona — Nomsa, CSI Manager:** Time-poor, comparing community investment options. Her goals are to identify a credible education partnership and report outcomes; pain points are vague impact claims, unclear costs, and slow responses. Give her transparent sponsorship options, clear next steps, and a prompt follow-up.

## Funnel

1. **Awareness:** Paid search, organic search, social stories, and partner referrals.
2. **Consideration:** Campaign landing page with mission, route/service information, impact evidence, sponsorship choices, and FAQs.
3. **Intent:** Sponsor CTA or qualified partnership enquiry.
4. **Conversion:** Completed sponsorship application, recorded in a secure production CRM.
5. **Nurture:** Permission-based welcome and impact emails; report outcomes and invite renewal.

## SEO and keyword strategy

- **High intent:** sponsor a learner South Africa; school transport sponsorship; sponsor a school transport route.
- **Local/service:** Eastern Cape rural school transport; safe learner transport Eastern Cape.
- **Partner intent:** education CSI projects South Africa; corporate social investment education.
- Use one descriptive title and meta description, clear headings, original helpful copy, meaningful internal links, descriptive alt text, mobile-friendly performance, and a verified canonical URL.
- Validate keyword demand and local search opportunities with live tools before spending. Avoid keyword stuffing. Configure Search Console and verify indexing after deployment.

## KPI and measurement plan

| KPI | Definition | Source/cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Completed applications | Count of valid completed applications | CRM; weekly and monthly |
| Qualified enquiry rate | Qualified enquiries / all enquiries | CRM qualification; weekly |
| Email delivery rate | Delivered / sent | Email provider; each send |
| Open rate | Unique opens / delivered | Email provider; directional only due privacy protections |
| Click-through rate | Unique email clicks / delivered | Email provider; per campaign/segment |
| Application conversion rate | Completed applications / attributed landing visits or delivered emails, report denominator explicitly | Analytics + CRM; weekly |
| Unsubscribe rate | Unsubscribes / delivered | Email provider; every send |
| Cost per qualified enquiry | Paid media spend / qualified enquiries from paid search | Ads + CRM; weekly |

Tag campaigns consistently (source, medium, campaign, content), deduplicate submissions, preserve consent, reconcile CRM conversions with analytics, monitor tracking errors daily, review channel/segment funnel weekly, and compare the six-month total with the prior six-month baseline monthly.

## A/B hypothesis

Variant B's safety-first headline and direct route-funding CTA will increase sponsor CTA clicks per assigned session by at least 15% relative to Variant A over four weeks. Split eligible new sessions evenly, keep form completion as a guardrail, and do not select a winner before a suitable sample size. The page's local prototype counters are not statistically valid campaign evidence.

## Sample results and one-page optimisation recommendation

**Illustrative values only:** 1,240 delivered, 38% opens (~471), 6.4% clicks (~79), and 2.1% applications (~26). These are modelled counts, not SchoolHop campaign results. The rates exceed the prototype review thresholds of 25%, 4%, and 2%; verify real platform definitions and actual outcome quality before interpreting performance.

1. Keep the current email as the control; test one segment-specific subject line and landing-page message at a time.
2. Improve sponsorship completion with clear route options, cost/next-step explanations, and a shorter form where possible.
3. Track qualified applications and cost per qualified enquiry, not just clicks; monitor unsubscribe rate and consent.
4. Confirm attribution and baseline data before deciding whether the 30% objective is met.
5. Increase spend only after tracking, privacy, safety, and lead quality pass review.
