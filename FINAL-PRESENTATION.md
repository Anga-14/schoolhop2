# SchoolHop Campaign — Final Presentation Outline

## Slide 1 — The challenge
- Rural learners in the Eastern Cape can face distance and safety barriers on the way to school.
- SchoolHop's purpose: support safe and reliable school transport.
- **Speaker note:** Use only verified, permission-cleared learner/community examples.

## Slide 2 — Audience and objective
- Primary audience: corporate CSI/ESG decision-makers.
- Secondary audiences: individual supporters, educators, and community partners.
- Objective: increase completed sponsorship applications by 30% in six months against the prior six-month baseline.

## Slide 3 — The digital journey
- Awareness → consideration → sponsorship intent → completed application → permission-based nurture.
- Show the responsive website, campaign CTA, contact/sponsor forms, and React Router navigation.
- State clearly that prototype submissions are stored only in the current browser.

## Slide 4 — Campaign materials
- SEO keyword groups and on-page recommendations.
- Proposed search ad and R6,000/four-week sample budget assumptions.
- One-week social calendar and five post drafts.
- Welcome and promotional email previews.

## Slide 5 — Measurement and learning
- KPIs: completed applications, qualified enquiry rate, email delivery/open/click, application conversion, unsubscribes, and paid cost per qualified enquiry.
- Measurement: daily tracking checks, weekly funnel review, monthly baseline comparison.
- Explain the A/B hypothesis and the importance of adequate sample size.

## Slide 6 — Dashboard interpretation
- Illustrative scenario: 1,240 delivered; 38% opens (~471); 6.4% clicks (~79); 2.1% applications (~26).
- These figures are sample data, not actual results.
- Recommendation: keep a control, test segment-specific messages, reduce application friction, and verify lead quality before increasing spend.

## Slide 7 — Testing, limitations, and launch gates
- Local checks: JavaScript syntax, form submissions, local counts, and dashboard recalculation.
- Limitations: no production API/CRM, no email credentials, no verified live campaign data, and Pages deployment is pending the first successful workflow run.
- Production use requires privacy/accessibility review, a secure backend, analytics/email setup, and end-to-end tests of those integrations.

## Slide 8 — Individual contribution and reflection
- Each student should state their own contribution, evidence, learning, and next improvement.
- Do not claim another person's work or present this template as a completed personal reflection.
